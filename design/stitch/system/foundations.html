Design Core Principles

  • No Official Seals: Authentic student civic utility. Strictly non-governmental visual cues.
  • Non-Tech-Savvy Accessibility: Clear visual hierarchies, tactile buttons, no low-contrast text.
  • Disciplined Geometry: 16px radius for surfaces, 10px for interactive controls, 1px Stone Grey borders.
  • No Generic SaaS Gimmicks: Zero glassmorphism, zero neon gradients, purposeful heritage hues.

Typography Scale & Bilingual Pairing

Inter + Noto Sans Devanagari • Strict Line Height Checks

Bilingual Hierarchy Rules

No thin weights (minimum 400 Regular). No heavy all-caps blocks. Devanagari line height is calibrated at 1.5–1.6 to prevent matra clipping.
