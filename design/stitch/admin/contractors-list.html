Maharashtra PWD Contractor Classification Rules

Contractors holding Class A accreditation are sanctioned for arterial highway works up to ₹15 Crores. Class B and C entities are restricted to Prabhag-level maintenance, pothole patching, and culvert reinstatement under SMKC Bye-laws 2018.
